The Sacred Ritual

A beginner's path to the three-card spread

01

Set the Sanctuary

Find a quiet, secluded area where your spirit feels at peace. You may light a candle or incense to cleanse the space and sharpen your focus.

02

Focus the Intention

Ask a simple, open question. Do not seek a bias; instead, ask "What can I learn from the coming moon?" and hold this thought while you shuffle.

03

The Trinity Draw

Divide the deck into three stacks. Flip the top card of each. From left to right, they represent your **Past**, your **Present**, and your **Future Outcome**.

History of Card

Pamela Colman Smith

"Pixie" • 1878 – 1951

This collection is an eternal tribute to the visionary work of Pamela Colman Smith. A British artist and occultist, her contributions redefined the language of the Tarot.

In 1909, she achieved a breakthrough: she was the first to illustrate the Minor Arcana with full human imagery, transforming symbols into deep stories.

Cat Prophet celebrates her original archetypes by re-imagining them through this dedicated cat-themed creative lens.
